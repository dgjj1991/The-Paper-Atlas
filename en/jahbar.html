Chapter 01

What Is This Place?

JahBar (家吧) is a live music bar on the Nan River in Chengdu's Wuhou District, founded in 2007 by a musician who went by "Lao Li" — a Sichuan northerner who played heavy guitar, fronted bands, and built reggae, ska-punk, and surf groups before deciding Chengdu needed a place like this.

The name is a pun: "家" means home, and "Jah" is Rastafarian for God (as in Reggae). The red, yellow, and green flag above the door is the logo. The philosophy is written on the wall: "For everyone who left their hometown — a place to feel at home in Chengdu."

JahBar is not a professional live house. It doesn't have the lighting rigs or acoustic isolation. What it has instead is something harder to find: no cover charge on weeknights, all ticket revenue from shows goes directly to the band, and a stage open to anyone who wants to play.

Chapter 02

The Story

Lao Li opened JahBar in 2007 on Hongmen Street by the old Nanmen Bridge, facing the Wan Li cruise boat on the Nan River. The bar was never supposed to become an institution. It just kept going.

The management team today includes founder Zhu Jie, and managers Yanzi and Tutu. The rules haven't changed: the bar takes nothing from ticket sales. All money goes to the performers. Free shows are free because the bar absorbs the cost.

JahBar has also opened a second location in Hainan, Sanya — Houhai Village in Haitang Bay — carrying surf, skateboard, and reggae culture to the coast.

Key moments

  • 2007 — Lao Li opens JahBar on Hongmen Street. The red-yellow-green flag goes up.
  • 2017 — Ten years in. JahBar is established as the gathering place for Chengdu's indie music scene.
  • 2019 — Twelfth anniversary. Bands JahWahZoo, Jie Wa, and others perform.
  • 2024+ — Seventeen years and running. Hongmen Street original going nightly.

Chapter 03

The Space

Inside

Centre Stage

Almost zero distance between performer and audience. When the band plays, you are close enough to feel the vibration of the strings.

Outside

Nan River Terrace

4–5 tables outside facing the Nan River. Foosball table. River water mixing with the reggae rhythms from inside.

The Vibe

Rough, Warm, Real

Walls covered in 17 years of band photos. Semi-self-serve bar culture.

Music

Live Jams

African drums, acoustic guitars, amplifiers around the stage ready for anyone to play.

Chapter 04

What's On

Check WeChat public account "JahbarMessage" before you go for the latest gig schedule.

  • Nightly open jam — Improvisational sets every evening without ticketed shows. Free entry.
  • Weekend headline shows — 5–6 per month on Friday/Saturday nights.
  • Open Stage — Bring your guitar or mic; perform and get encouraged.

Chapter 07

Insider Tips

What regulars know

  • 01
    Check WeChat "JahbarMessage" beforehand Know whether tonight is a free jam night or a ticketed headline show.
  • 02
    Start outside by the river, then move inside Have your first cold beer on the Nan River terrace before the live music heats up indoors.
  • 03
    Semi-self-serve bar Walk up to the bar counter, order your drink, and pay. Everyone is equal here.
