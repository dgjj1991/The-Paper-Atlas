Chapter 01

What Is This Place?

Maan Coffee (漫咖啡) is a Korean-style "slow living" cafe chain launched in China in 2011. The Jinjiang branch is situated along Section 3 of Renmin South Road, right behind the historic Jinjiang Hotel, between Nanmen Bridge and Huaxiba.

The Chinese name "漫" (Màn) plays on romance and slowness. Rather than quick grab-and-go espresso, Maan Coffee was built as a spacious "Third Space" — a comfortable haven outside home and work where you can sit, chat, work, or dream for hours.

Chapter 02

The Story

Founded in 2011 by Korean restaurateur Guram Guram/Xin Zixiang, Maan Coffee pioneered the Korean cafe aesthetic in China: double-height ceilings, recycled elm timber, raw steel, crystal chandeliers, indoor trees, and freshly baked waffles.

One signature touch remains iconic: when you order, you are handed a colorful **teddy bear** to place on your table. The server uses it to locate you, and takes it away once your food arrives.

Insider Tips

What regulars know

  • 01
    Combine with JahBar at night Spend a quiet afternoon at Maan Coffee, then walk 10 minutes along the Nan River to JahBar for evening live reggae music — a perfect day-to-night Chengdu route.
  • 02
    Expect atmosphere, not single-origin pour-overs Maan Coffee excels at space, seating comfort, and waffles — it's not a specialty third-wave coffee shop.
