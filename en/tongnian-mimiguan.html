Chapter 01

What Is This Place?

Tucked into an alleyway in Yulin, this signless 30㎡ glass house is home to thousands of archived secrets written by people born between 1947 and 2019. Founded by curator Hu Yanzi and artist Zhao Xuetong, it's a quiet sanctuary of human connection where you can read strangers' memories or write your own.
