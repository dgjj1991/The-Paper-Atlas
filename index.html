Youxing Bookstore

成都有杏书店

An independent bookstore hiding inside a 1970s warehouse — where Chengdu's intellectual scene actually meets. Less Instagram bait, more honest conversation. And the cheapest good coffee in the building.

📍 Erxianqiao · Chenghua 🕙 09:30 – 17:30 👤 ~¥24

Teahouse · Culture ¥

Jinqin Old Teahouse

成都金琴老茶馆

A 300-year-old Qing Dynasty farmhouse relocated to a Chengdu neighbourhood. ¥10 tea, unlimited refills, free Peking opera. The most honest deal in the city.

📍 Fuqin · Jinniu District 🕗 08:00 – 18:00 👤 ~¥18

JahBar

家吧 JahBar

17 years on the Nan River. No cover charge on weeknights. All ticket money goes to the band. Punk, reggae, jazz, whoever shows up — live every night.

📍 Hongmen St · Wuhou 🌙 19:30 – 03:00 👤 ~¥40–62
